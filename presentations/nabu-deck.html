nabuProject & business overview / 01
Delivery risk reviewer

Better plans.
Grounded in
past delivery.

Nabu reviews a new epic against the team’s ticket history and surfaces potential risks with cited evidence.

The business opportunity

Teams already own the evidence.
It is buried in old tickets.

Delivery leads and engineering managers need to catch missing work and dependencies before committing to a plan.

Initial customer hypothesis: organisations with 50–500 engineers and consultancies managing repeat delivery.

Proposed buying reason
Less time reconstructing past lessons. Earlier decisions on delivery risks.
Project basis: README.md and plan.md. Customer demand and willingness to pay remain to be validated.

nabuBusiness model / 03

A subscription model with
measurable delivery costs

Proposed company subscription
£750 / month

Illustrative offer for one delivery organisation with a defined review allowance. Charge separately for onboarding and unusually high usage.

Value to test: at an assumed £75 per staff hour, 10 hours saved per month equals the subscription price.

Operating break-even: 40 customers
£24,000 fixed monthly costs ÷ £600 contribution.
Illustrative monthly economics per customer
Subscription revenue£750
Model calls & infrastructure£100
Support delivery allowance£50
Gross contribution£600 / 80%

At 50 customers: £37,500 revenue, £30,000 gross contribution and £6,000 operating surplus per month under these assumptions.

Adoption, sales costs and support intensity determine whether these margins hold.

Planning scenario only. All prices, costs and savings are assumptions, not actual results. Fixed costs include assumed payroll, product development and sales/admin. Excludes tax, financing and one-off setup costs.
nabuPath to market / 04

The next milestone is a
paid, evidence-backed pilot

01 / Establish quality

Prove useful warnings

Evaluate the current agent team on held-out historical plans, then run prospective reviews with delivery leads.

Measure: useful warnings, false positives and reviewer time.

02 / Establish value

Recruit design partners

Propose three paid pilots using authorised tracker exports. Compare planning effort and actions taken before and after each review.

Measure: willingness to pay and repeat use.

03 / Prepare production

Make company rollout viable

Add SSO and server-side access controls, protected storage and retention controls. Build live tracker connectors around validated demand.

Measure: onboarding effort and cost per review.

Expansion gate: useful findings, repeat usage and positive contribution margin. Validate forecast calibration before selling delivery-date guarantees.
Proposed commercial roadmap. Nabu-2 implementation basis: docs/agents.md, docs/interface.md and nabu/agents/writer.py.
