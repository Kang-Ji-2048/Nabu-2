Delivery risk reviewer

Better plans.
Grounded in
past delivery.

Nabu reviews a new epic against the team’s ticket history and surfaces potential risks with cited evidence.

The business opportunity

Teams already own the evidence.
It is buried in old tickets.

Delivery leads and engineering managers need to catch missing work and dependencies before committing to a plan.

Initial customer hypothesis: organisations with 50–500 engineers and consultancies managing repeat delivery.

Proposed buying reason
Less time reconstructing past lessons. Earlier decisions on delivery risks.
Project basis: README.md and plan.md. Customer demand and willingness to pay remain to be validated.
nabuHow it works / 02

How Nabu reviews a plan

  1. 01

    History

    The historian finds patterns in past tickets.

    Scoped to the team and review date
  2. 02

    Specialists

    Check estimates, dependencies and missing work.

    The planner routes the new plan
  3. 03

    Critic

    Challenge claims and check their citations.

    Accept, revise once, or drop
  4. 04

    Review

    Publish risks and suggested tickets.

    The delivery lead decides what changes
After delivery

The scorekeeper checks the warnings and updates pattern weights for the next review.

Python supplies evidence and predictions. Optional Claude calls phrase and critique them. Forecasts are historical estimates, not delivery guarantees.
nabuAgent design / 03

Build it. Let it run.
Prove it worked.

01

Build the agent

Python orchestrates specialists and a critic through the Claude Messages API.

02

Define the finish line

A saved review with actionable findings. Every warning needs at least two known, in-scope citations.

03

Let it run safely

Saved checkpoints, request and step limits, one retry, then deterministic fallback.

04

Build the evaluation

Score warnings against outcomes. Compare forecast error and coverage with a historical baseline.

05

Improve the next run

Hits and misses adjust pattern weights. Next: test prompt changes on held-out cases and revert regressions.

Adapted from the supplied event slide. Implemented: custom Python workflow, durable state, outcome scoring and baseline evaluation. Managed Agents, /goal and automated prompt hill climbing are not currently integrated. Plan changes stay with the delivery lead.
